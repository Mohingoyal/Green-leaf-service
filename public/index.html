Our Services

We serve as a common platform for people who wish to contribute
and those seeking support, especially in the field of medical supplies.

Our work is guided by the fundamental principle of humanity—
to alleviate the suffering of those in need whenever and wherever possible,
while ensuring that every individual is treated with dignity, compassion,
and respect.

At Green Leaf, donors can contribute medicines and medical equipment
such as wheelchairs, arm slings, walking sticks, and many other essential items,
whether new or gently used. Items that may no longer be useful to the donor
can make a significant difference in the lives of people who cannot afford these
supplies and are in urgent need of medical assistance.

Anyone in need of medical supplies can browse through the list of donated
items as well as equipment available for sale, with search results tailored
to their city. Green Leaf also provides information about NGOs operating
in the user's city, enabling them to connect with these organizations
to find the medicines and medical equipment they require.

After completing registration using government-issued verification documents,
NGOs can browse medicines and medical equipment listed for donation or sale,
with search results tailored to their locality. They can procure the required
quantities of these supplies for fair, responsible, and transparent distribution
to individuals and communities in need.
